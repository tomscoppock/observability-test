Business Expenses Policy
Effective Date: 1 April 2025
Version: 3.0

1. Purpose

This policy sets out the rules for claiming reimbursement of expenses incurred while conducting company business. All employees are expected to exercise good judgement and spend company money as if it were their own.

2. General Principles

Expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and incurred wholly for business purposes. The most cost-effective option should always be chosen. Extravagant or lavish spending will not be reimbursed.

All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be processed except in exceptional circumstances approved by the Finance Director.

3. Receipts

Original receipts or digital copies are required for all expenses over GBP 25. For incidental expenses of GBP 25 or under, a brief description of the expense is sufficient without a receipt.

Credit card statements alone are not acceptable as proof of expenditure. Itemised receipts must be provided.

4. Travel

Rail travel should be booked in standard class. First class travel is only permitted for journeys over 2 hours with prior manager approval. Advance tickets should be purchased where possible to reduce costs.

Mileage for use of personal vehicles is reimbursed at HMRC approved rates: 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles, then 25p per mile thereafter. Employees must hold valid business insurance for their vehicle.

Air travel must be booked in economy class for domestic and short-haul flights. Business class may be approved for long-haul flights over 6 hours at the discretion of the department head.

5. Accommodation

Hotel accommodation should not exceed GBP 150 per night in London and GBP 120 per night elsewhere in the UK. International rates should be reasonable for the destination city.

Bookings should be made through the company's preferred booking platform where possible. Loyalty programme points earned on business travel belong to the employee.

6. Meals and Entertainment

Daily meal allowances when travelling on business: breakfast GBP 10, lunch GBP 15, dinner GBP 30. Alcohol is not reimbursable except when entertaining clients, subject to a reasonable limit.

Client entertainment expenses must be pre-approved by a line manager for amounts over GBP 100. The names and company affiliations of all attendees must be recorded.

7. Technology and Equipment

Purchases of technology equipment (laptops, monitors, software licences) must be approved by IT and the employee's line manager before purchase. Items over GBP 500 require Finance Director approval.

8. Submission Process

Expenses should be submitted through the company expenses portal with scanned receipts attached. Claims are reviewed by the employee's line manager and processed by Finance within 10 working days of approval. Reimbursement is made via the monthly payroll.
