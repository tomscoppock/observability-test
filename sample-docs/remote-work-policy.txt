Remote and Hybrid Working Policy
Effective Date: 1 March 2025
Version: 1.3

1. Overview

The company operates a hybrid working model that balances flexibility with collaboration. This policy applies to all employees whose roles can be performed remotely, as determined by their department head.

2. Hybrid Working Arrangements

Employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week, with the remaining 3 days spent in the office. The specific remote days should be agreed with the employee's line manager and kept consistent where possible to support team planning.

Core office days are Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Employees are expected to be in the office on at least two of these three days. Monday and Friday are designated as flexible days when remote work is encouraged.

3. Home Office Requirements

Employees working from home must ensure they have a suitable workspace that meets basic health and safety requirements. The company will provide a one-time home office allowance of GBP 300 for equipment such as a monitor, keyboard, or ergonomic chair.

A stable internet connection with a minimum speed of 10 Mbps download is required. Employees experiencing connectivity issues should work from the office on affected days.

4. Working Hours and Availability

Remote workers must be available during core hours (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM) and responsive on company communication channels (Slack, email, Teams). Total working hours should align with the employee's contracted hours.

Employees must not work from locations outside the United Kingdom for more than 20 working days per year without prior approval from HR, due to tax and employment law implications.

5. Equipment and Security

Company-issued laptops must be used for all work activities. Personal devices may not be used to access company systems unless approved by IT Security. VPN must be active when accessing internal systems remotely.

Employees must ensure that confidential information is not visible to others when working in public spaces. Screen privacy filters are available from IT on request.

6. Requesting Changes

Employees wishing to change their hybrid working pattern should submit a request through the HR portal. Requests will be reviewed within 10 working days. Changes to working patterns may be subject to a trial period of 3 months.

7. Right to Disconnect

The company respects employees' right to disconnect outside of working hours. Managers should not expect responses to non-urgent communications sent outside of core hours or during weekends and bank holidays.
