Employee Code of Conduct
Effective Date: 1 January 2025
Version: 4.2

1. Purpose and Scope

This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour expected of all employees, contractors, and temporary workers. It applies during working hours, at company events, and in any situation where an individual is representing the company.

2. Professional Behaviour

All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally and treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and courtesy. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or victimisation of any kind will not be tolerated.

The company is committed to providing an inclusive workplace free from discrimination based on age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, or sexual orientation.

3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must declare any personal, financial, or family interests that could conflict with their duties. This includes outside employment, directorships, significant shareholdings in competitors or suppliers, and personal relationships with clients or vendors.

Declarations should be made to the employee's line manager and HR in writing. The company will assess each situation and may impose restrictions to manage the conflict.

4. Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company information, including trade secrets, financial data, client information, and strategic plans. Confidential information must not be shared with unauthorised persons, whether inside or outside the company.

The obligation of confidentiality continues after employment ends, as set out in the employee's contract of employment.

5. Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, systems, and resources are provided for business use. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, incur significant cost, or breach any company policy.

Employees must not use company systems to access, store, or distribute inappropriate, offensive, or illegal material.

6. Social Media

Employees are free to use social media in a personal capacity but must not make statements that could be interpreted as representing the company's views. Company confidential information must never be shared on social media.

When identifying themselves as company employees on professional platforms (e.g. LinkedIn), employees should make clear that views expressed are their own.

7. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept modest gifts and hospitality in the normal course of business, up to a value of GBP 50. Gifts or hospitality exceeding this value must be declared to the employee's line manager.

Cash gifts must never be accepted. Gifts from suppliers during procurement processes must be declined.

8. Disciplinary Process

Breaches of this Code of Conduct will be investigated and may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. The severity of the sanction will depend on the nature and circumstances of the breach.

Minor breaches may result in a verbal warning. Repeated or more serious breaches may lead to written warnings. Gross misconduct, including fraud, theft, violence, or serious harassment, may result in summary dismissal without notice.

9. Reporting Concerns

Employees who witness or become aware of breaches of this Code should report them to their line manager, HR, or through the confidential whistleblowing hotline. The company prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports concerns in good faith.
