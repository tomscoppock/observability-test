Annual Leave and Time Off Policy
Effective Date: 1 January 2025
Version: 2.1

1. Annual Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public bank holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours.

New starters who join partway through the year will receive a pro-rata entitlement for the remainder of the calendar year. Unused leave cannot be carried over to the next year except in exceptional circumstances approved by the employee's line manager and HR.

2. Booking Leave

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for periods of one week or more, and at least three working days in advance for shorter absences. Managers should respond to leave requests within two working days.

During peak periods (July, August, and December), no more than 30% of a team may be on leave simultaneously. Requests during these periods should be submitted at least four weeks in advance.

3. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences of three or more consecutive days, a medical certificate (fit note) is required. Sick leave beyond the paid allowance will be handled under the company's long-term sickness policy.

Employees must notify their line manager by 9:30 AM on the first day of absence, either by phone or email.

4. Parental Leave

Maternity leave: 26 weeks at full pay, followed by 13 weeks at statutory maternity pay, and a further 13 weeks unpaid. Paternity leave: 4 weeks at full pay within the first 8 weeks of the child's birth or adoption. Shared parental leave is available in accordance with statutory provisions.

5. Compassionate Leave

Up to 5 days of paid compassionate leave is available for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, parent, child, sibling). Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the discretion of the line manager.

6. Other Leave Types

Jury service: Full pay for the duration of jury service, minus any court allowance received. Public duties: Reasonable time off for magistrates, school governors, and similar roles. Study leave: Up to 5 days per year for approved professional development courses.
